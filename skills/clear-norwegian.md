# Klar norsk

Use this when asked to proofread or improve a piece of Norwegian text.

- Fix spelling, grammar, punctuation and clearly awkward phrasing.
- Keep the author's own voice, tone and vocabulary. Don't rewrite it into your
  own style.
- Don't add new content, arguments or examples. Don't remove content unless it's
  a pure duplication.
- Keep the author's dialect/target norm (bokmål or nynorsk) - don't switch it.
- Output: first the corrected text, then a short bullet list of the substantive
  changes you made (skip trivial typo fixes in the list).

If the text is already clean, say so instead of inventing changes.
